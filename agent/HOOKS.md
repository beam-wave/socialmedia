# Hook Playbook: comments and follows

Instagram ranks posts on **shares, saves, comments and watch time**, roughly in that order. A follow happens when someone expects *more of the same tomorrow*. Every post therefore needs three hooks:

1. **A stop hook:** the first line on slide 1 or in the caption, which stops the scroll.
2. **A comment hook:** a question that is easy and quick to answer, and fun to argue about.
3. **A follow hook:** a specific reason to come back, such as Part 2, tomorrow's post, a streak, or the result.

All five templates already include a comment question on the last slide and in the caption, plus a **Comment & Follow** slide. Click **New comment hook** in any studio to rotate questions.

---

## 1. Comment hooks that work, and why

| Type | Example | Why people comment |
|---|---|---|
| **This or that** | "Texts first or waits?" | One-word answers, so there's almost no effort |
| **Pick a number** | "Comment 01–05" | Feels like voting, and people defend their pick |
| **Predict** | "Hit or flop? We'll check Monday" | People want to be proven right, so they come back |
| **Rate it** | "Rate this out of 10" | Quick, and everyone has an opinion |
| **Fill the blank** | "Love is…" | Lets people express themselves; answers get long |
| **Tag someone** | "Tag the one who…" | Each tag brings a new viewer, which helps reach |
| **Confession** | "What's stopping you? Be honest" | Personal, so replies get longer |
| **Keyword-to-DM** | "Comment LIST and we'll DM the links" | A big comment boost (needs a DM auto-reply tool such as ManyChat) |
| **Pledge** | "Type I'M IN if you're showing up" | Public commitment, and it repeats daily |
| **Quiz** | "Answer like 1A 2B 3A" | Gamified, and people check replies to compare |
| **Gentle debate** | "Overhyped or underrated?" | Disagreement brings replies to replies |
| **Pinned reward** | "Closest guess gets pinned" | A small status prize |

**Rules:**

- Ask one question per post.
- Keep it answerable in under 5 seconds.
- Reply to the first 20 comments within an hour; it doubles the thread.
- Pin the best comment.

## 2. Follow hooks (give a reason, not just "follow us")

- **Series:** "Part 1 of 3. Follow for Part 2 tomorrow."
- **Streak:** "Day 43 / 365. Follow so you don't break the streak." (@_motivation2live__)
- **Payoff later:** "Follow for the score summary right after the match." (@mid.innings)
- **Schedule:** "New domain every Monday. Next week: AI Agents." (@spotlight.startups)
- **First to know:** "Every new poster and teaser, the day it drops." (@bollyvibe)
- **Daily ritual:** "A little love in your feed every day." (@puree_heartz)
- **Callback:** "We'll check your predictions on Monday." This gives people a reason to return to the profile.

## 3. Stop hooks (first line of slide 1 or the caption)

- "Nobody talks about this…"
- "5 startups quietly rebuilding X"
- "Save this for the days you need it"
- "Read this if you're tired of starting over"
- "This one is for the person who stayed"
- "Predict before you swipe"
- "The teaser everyone's talking about"
- "Only 1% will finish this streak"
- "You'll want to send this to someone"

## 5. Habits that grow followers
- **Post at the same time daily.** People follow a schedule.
- **Cross-promote:** mention sister channels in stories (for example, @_motivation2live__ ↔ @puree_heartz).
- **Run a story poll** with the same question as the post, then share the results the next day. That's a second touchpoint.
- **Make the last slide the Comment & Follow slide,** and on the second-to-last slide say "answer on the next slide".
- **Turn the best comments into a post.** For example: "You said ___ was the turning point."
- **Avoid engagement bait** that Instagram demotes, such as "like if…" or "comment YES to win". Real questions are fine.
