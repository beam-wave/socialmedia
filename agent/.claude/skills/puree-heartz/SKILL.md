# @puree_heartz: heartly couple quotes (EN + HI)

**Format:** 2 slides: quote card, then Comment & Follow. The app renders them from `post.quotes[0]`.

**Voice:** warm, intimate, quietly romantic. Think of a line someone sends to their person without saying anything. No cheesy clichés, no heartbreak bitterness, no sexual content.

## Steps
1. Read the recent posts in the prompt and pick a different emotion. Rotate through comfort, silence, small gestures, long distance, choosing each other, home, patience, firsts, and gratitude.
2. Write `quote`: one original English line, **under 25 words**. Wrap one punch phrase in `*stars*`.
3. Write `quote_hi`: the same feeling in natural Hindi/Urdu-flavoured Devanagari (shayari tone welcome). Under 25 words, with its own `*highlight*`. It doesn't have to be a literal translation.
4. Optional `note` / `note_hi`: a tiny line under the signature, like "send this to your home".
5. Leave `author` empty for original lines. Only quote a real person if you verified it with WebSearch, and then add the source.
6. Set `theme` to rotate moods: blush, midnight, petal, dusk. Don't repeat the most recent post's theme.
7. Choose a `hook` from the bank (with `q_hi`). Prefer share/tag hooks; they spread reach.

## Hook bank
- Tag the one who *understands your silence*. / उसे टैग करो जो *तुम्हारी ख़ामोशी* समझता है।
- *Send this* to them without saying a word.
- Describe your person in *one word*. / अपने ख़ास इंसान को *एक शब्द* में बताओ।
- Who said *I love you* first? options: Me / Them / Still waiting
- Texts first or *waits for their text*? options: I text first / I wait
- Long distance or *same city*? options: Long distance / Same city
- Complete the line: *Love is…*
- Comment your *initials + theirs*.
- How long have you been *together*?

`sources` can be empty for original lines.
