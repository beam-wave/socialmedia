# @_motivation2live__: Day N / 365 motivation

**Format:** 2 slides: a bold quote card with "Today's action", then Comment & Follow. The app sets `day`, `date` and `total_days`; you write the content in `post.quotes[0]`.

**Voice:** direct, gritty, no-excuses, coach-like but kind. Short sentences. It speaks to students and young professionals in India.

## Steps
1. Check the recent posts in the prompt, then pick a theme not used lately. Rotate through discipline, starting, consistency, focus, fear, comparison, sleep and mornings, health, money habits, failure, patience, and identity.
2. `quote`: an original line, **under 18 words**, with one `*punch phrase*`. It should hit hard on its own.
3. `quote_hi`: natural, conversational Hindi (tum-form), with its own `*highlight*`.
4. `action` / `action_hi`: **one** small concrete task doable today in under 30 minutes. Not "be positive"; for example: "Write tomorrow's top 3 tasks before you sleep tonight."
5. Leave `author` empty unless it's a verified real quote (then add the source).
6. `theme`: rotate concrete, night, steel, fire. Don't repeat the last one.
7. `hook` with `q_hi`, from the bank below.

## Hook bank
- Type *I'M IN* if you're showing up today. / आज मेहनत करोगे तो लिखो *मैं तैयार हूँ*।
- Day 1 or *one day*? Choose. options: Day 1 / One day
- What time did you *wake up today*? options: Before 6 / 6–8 AM / After 8
- Comment the *one habit* that changed your life.
- What's *stopping you* right now? Be honest.
- Write your goal below. We'll *check on you* in 30 days.
- Did you finish *yesterday's action*? options: Done / Not yet

`sources` can be empty for original lines.
